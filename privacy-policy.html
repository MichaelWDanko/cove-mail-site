Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Cove Mail handles information in the macOS app, licensing service, website, and support workflow.

Information You Add to Cove Mail

Cove Mail may use account names, email addresses, server settings, mailbox names, message metadata, draft content, sent messages, message flags, search index data, and other mail content needed to provide the email client experience.

Credential Storage

Cove Mail is designed to store account passwords through macOS Keychain. Local app configuration stores account metadata and Keychain references rather than raw passwords.

Local-First Storage

Cove Mail is intended to keep cached metadata, account configuration, draft state, and review decisions on your Mac unless you explicitly export or move that data.

Local Automation

Cove Mail may expose local automation tools for compatible clients. These tools are run through local transports and use account and capability permissions that you can change in Cove Mail. A connected assistant may receive the mail data you ask Cove Mail to share.

Licensing and Purchases

The licensing service uses the email address associated with a purchase or complimentary license, subscription and transaction identifiers, plan and status information, activation claims, and device activation identifiers. It does not need mailbox content to issue or verify a license.

Paddle is the merchant of record and processes checkout, payment, tax, receipts, subscription management, cancellations, and refunds. Cove Mail does not receive or store complete payment card details.

Service Providers

Cove Mail uses service providers to operate its website, licensing database and API, authentication, payment processing, and transactional email. These providers process the information needed to perform those services under their own terms and privacy notices.

Tracking and Advertising

Cove Mail does not sell mail data or use mail content for advertising. The public website loads Paddle when it prepares localized pricing or checkout. Paddle may use cookies or similar technology as described in its privacy notice.

Your Choices

  • You control which accounts are added to Cove Mail.
  • You can remove account configuration from the app.
  • You can manage stored credentials in macOS Keychain.
  • You can choose whether to use local automation features when available.
  • You can manage or cancel a paid subscription through Paddle.
  • You can contact support about licensing information associated with your email.

Retention and Security

Local mail data remains on your Mac until you remove it or reset Cove Mail. Licensing, purchase, audit, and delivery records are retained as needed to provide access, prevent abuse, support customers, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Cove Mail uses access controls, encrypted connections, secret storage, and signed entitlements to protect the hosted licensing system.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email covemailapp@gmail.com.
