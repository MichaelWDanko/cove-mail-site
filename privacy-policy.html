Cove Mail

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

Cove Mail is a Mac email app operated by Michael Danko. This policy explains how information is handled by the app, website, licensing service, payment service, and customer support.

What This Policy Covers

This policy covers Cove Mail and the services we operate for subscriptions, activation, and support. Your email provider, payment provider, and any AI assistant you connect have their own privacy policies. You should review those policies before using their services.

Information Stored on Your Mac

Cove Mail stores email account settings, mailbox and message data, attachments you keep offline, search indexes, drafts, Outbox items, rules, activity history, and AI connection settings on your Mac. The app uses this information to display, search, organize, and send email.

Cove Mail connects directly to the email providers you configure. We do not operate a hosted copy of your mailbox. Passwords and authentication tokens are stored in macOS Keychain, and the app stores references to those credentials instead of the credentials themselves in its other local files.

Trial Information

On first launch, Cove Mail creates a random installation identifier in macOS Keychain. After the first successful email sync, it stores the trial start date, expiration date, and most recent date used with that identifier. This information stays on your Mac and is not sent to the licensing service. Cove Mail does not use a device fingerprint to run the trial.

Subscriptions and Licenses

For paid and complimentary access, we use the email address associated with the license, subscription and transaction identifiers, plan and status information, activation records, and random device identifiers. We use this information to issue, activate, recover, renew, and protect licenses. The licensing service does not need your email account credentials or email content.

Paddle is the merchant of record for Cove Mail subscriptions. Paddle processes checkout, payment, tax, receipts, subscription changes, cancellations, and refunds. We receive the purchase and subscription details needed to provide access, but we do not receive or store your complete payment card number. Paddle handles payment information under its Privacy Notice.

Website Information and Cookies

When you visit covemail.ai or download Cove Mail, the companies that host the site and download may process routine request information such as your IP address, browser type, requested page, and date and time. They use this information to deliver and protect their services.

The home page loads Paddle to display prices and provide checkout and subscription management. Paddle may use cookies or similar technology for those services. Cove Mail does not currently use a separate website analytics service or advertising trackers, and we do not use email content for advertising.

AI Assistants You Connect

Cove Mail does not include an AI model. If you connect an external AI assistant, you choose the email accounts and actions it can use. The assistant may receive email data returned by the actions you enable. Once an action is enabled, the assistant can run it without another approval from Cove Mail. The company that provides the assistant is responsible for how it handles the information it receives.

Support and Diagnostics

If you contact support, we receive your email address and the information you include in the request. Cove Mail does not send diagnostic reports automatically. If you use the diagnostic tools in the app, you can review the report before you copy, save, or email it. Do not send account passwords, authentication tokens, activation links, or private email content to support.

How We Use Hosted Information

We use information held by our online services to:

  • process subscriptions and provide paid or complimentary access;
  • send activation, recovery, billing, and service messages;
  • answer support and privacy requests;
  • protect the licensing service and prevent fraud or misuse; and
  • keep records required for tax, accounting, disputes, and other legal duties.

Where the law requires a legal basis, these uses are needed to perform our agreement with you, meet legal obligations, protect our legitimate interests in operating a secure service, or act with your consent.

Who Handles Hosted Information

We use service providers to host the website and downloads, maintain licensing records, process payments, and deliver activation and support email. Current providers include GitHub, Amazon Web Services, Supabase, Resend, Google, and Paddle. They process information for the services they provide under their own agreements and privacy notices.

We may disclose information when required by law, to protect people or the service, or as part of a sale or transfer of Cove Mail. We do not sell personal information.

Retention and Deletion

Information stored by the app stays on your Mac until you remove an email account or use the app's option to delete local Cove Mail data. Deleting local data does not delete messages or other information held by your email provider. The local trial identifier and dates remain in Keychain after local data is deleted so that deleting and reinstalling the app does not restart the trial.

We keep licensing and subscription records while access is active and afterward when needed for support, security, fraud prevention, accounting, tax, disputes, and legal obligations. We keep support messages only as long as they are useful for the request or a related legal obligation. Our providers may keep their own records under their retention policies.

Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You can choose which email accounts and AI assistant actions to enable. You can remove email accounts, clear AI activity history, delete local Cove Mail data, cancel subscription renewal, and choose whether to send a diagnostic report.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you. You may also have rights to receive a copy, restrict or object to some uses, withdraw consent, or complain to a privacy authority. Email us to make a request. We may need to confirm your identity, and some records must be kept when required by law. Requests about Paddle's records should also be sent to Paddle.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information handled by Cove Mail and its online services. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

Cove Mail is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information through our online services. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information so we can review and delete it when appropriate.

International Processing

Our service providers may process hosted information in the United States and other countries. Privacy protections can differ by country. Where required, the provider or Cove Mail uses an approved legal method for the transfer.

Changes to This Policy

We will post an updated policy on this page and change the effective date when the policy changes. We will provide additional notice when required by law.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email covemailapp@gmail.com. Cove Mail is operated by Michael Danko.
