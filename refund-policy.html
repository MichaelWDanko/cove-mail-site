Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026

Paddle is the merchant of record for Cove Mail purchases and handles subscription cancellations and refunds.

14-Day Local Trial

Cove Mail's 14-day local trial does not require payment information and does not create a charge. No cancellation or refund request is needed when the trial ends.

Cancel a Subscription

You can cancel renewal at any time through the Paddle customer portal linked from the Cove Mail website or your purchase receipt. Cancellation normally takes effect at the end of the current billing period, and Paddle will not charge the next renewal.

Request a Refund

Request a refund through your Paddle receipt, the Paddle customer portal, or Paddle Buyer Support. Eligibility and processing follow Paddle's Refund Policy and any mandatory rights under applicable law. Approved refunds are returned through Paddle to the original payment method when possible.

Technical Problems

If a technical problem prevents you from using Cove Mail as described, email covemailapp@gmail.com so support can try to resolve it. If the problem cannot be resolved, request a refund through Paddle. A refund ends the corresponding paid access.

Mandatory Rights

This policy does not limit consumer rights that cannot be waived. If local law gives you stronger cancellation, withdrawal, or refund rights, those rights apply.
