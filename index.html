Inbox compatibility

Works with the inboxes you already use.

Connect iCloud Mail, Gmail, and manually configured IMAP and SMTP accounts. Switch between accounts and mailboxes in one interface.

  • ✓ Keep each email account separate
  • ✓ Search one account or all accounts
  • ✓ See sync status for each account

Stored on your Mac

Cove Mail does not keep a hosted copy of your mailbox.

Cove Mail keeps your email data, account information, and preferences on your Mac. Sign-in credentials are protected using macOS Keychain. A connected AI assistant receives only data allowed by the settings you choose.

The email app underneath

Use Cove Mail for everyday email.

Cove Mail remains a complete Mac email app when no AI assistant is connected.

01

Compose, save drafts, reply, forward, organize mail, add attachments, and send from the account and signature you choose.

Keep selected mailboxes, messages, and attachments available offline. See sync, indexing, Outbox, and recovery status in the app.
